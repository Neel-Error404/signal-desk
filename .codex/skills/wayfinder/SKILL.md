# Wayfinder

Produce a governed intake packet before architecture or implementation.

## Workflow

1. State the requested outcome and the affected users.
2. Separate success conditions, failure conditions, constraints, and non-goals.
3. Identify repository topology, lifecycle, risk, data sensitivity, and autonomy ceiling.
4. Identify accountable product, architecture, security, and release owners.
5. Select capability packs and authoritative knowledge sources.
6. Record unresolved questions instead of fabricating answers.
7. Hand the packet to `grill-me` or `grill-with-docs` when material ambiguity remains.

## Output

```text
Objective:
Success:
Failure:
Constraints:
Non-goals:
Risk:
Authority:
Capability packs:
Knowledge sources:
Open questions:
Next proof gate:
```

Do not authorize execution. Intake establishes the information required for a governed decision.
