# Learning Promotion

## Workflow

1. Cluster relevant evidence without changing trusted state.
2. Form the weakest sufficient hypothesis.
3. Replay the hypothesis in an isolated environment.
4. Test counterexamples and regression cases.
5. Draft a candidate memory, skill, policy, evaluator, or ADR.
6. Run independent evaluation and shadow mode.
7. Require kind-specific accountable approval and a separate promoter.
8. Emit an immutable promotion packet without applying the target mutation.
9. Hand the packet to the target-specific governed workflow for application, canary, observation,
   or rollback when that workflow exists.

Learning agents cannot directly modify production, constitutional policy, trusted evaluators, or
trusted memory. A P4.6 promotion packet is evidence and authority routing, not the mutation itself.
